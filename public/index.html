FRAGRANCE MASTERCLASS

How To Maximize Sillage & Longevity

01

Moisturize Before Spraying

Fragrance molecules adhere far better to hydrated skin. Apply an unscented lotion or petroleum jelly on pulse points.

02

Target Warm Pulse Points

Spray on wrists, sides of the neck, and behind knees. Body heat gently activates and radiates the perfume all day.

03

Never Rub Your Wrists

Friction crushes the fragile top notes (citrus & florals), altering the evolution of the scent pyramid.
